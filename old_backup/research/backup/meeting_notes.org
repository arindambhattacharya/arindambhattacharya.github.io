* February 4, 2019
*** CA-LBF Algorithm
- Given initial train data $S_0$, we train a classifier model $C_0$
- On insertion, a batch of temporary elements $S_i$ are stored temporarily. When size of $S_i$ reaches a threshold, we train a classifier $C_i$ **incrementally**. That is, we take the parameters of classifier $C_{i-1}$ and train it on $S_i$.
- On query, an example is positive if any of the classifier gives positive. Hence the increase in FPR in the [[file:img/fpr_methods_nobase.pdf][plot]].

Note: All our data (both positive and negative) currently comes from the same distribution. The distribution deesn't vary over time.

*** Alternative Algorithm
- Given initial train data $S_0$, we train a classifier model $C_0$
- On insertion, a batch of temporary elements $S_i$ are stored temporarily. When size of $S_i$ reaches a threshold, we train a **new** classifier $C_i$ on $S_i$ only.
- Query is same.


*** Advantages of second method
- Adapt to changing distribution of data. Changes in first method is minimum. Will not adapt to new distribution fast.

*** Issues with second method
- Not enough data to train unless the batch threshold is considerably increased, thus largly increasing the training time.
- Classifiers are not meant to adapt to changing distribution (iid assumption). Will not be able to predict change.

*** Potential solution
- Reinforment learning
- Time series analysis
