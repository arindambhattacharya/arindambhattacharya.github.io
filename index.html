Biography

I am a PhD student in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi. I work at the intersection of Machine Learning and Compact Data Structures with focus on Learning Theoretic analysis, advised by Prof. Amitabha Bagchi and Prof. Srikanta Bedathur.
