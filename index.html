Biography

I am an Applied Scientist and Senior Software Engineer with 10+ years of experience building large-scale production AI systems. Currently at Google (Core Data Foundations, Bangalore), I am architecting a petabyte-scale Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) system for enterprise analytics. Previously at Amazon Search Science and AI, I led research in multilingual semantic retrieval, efficient LLM training, and knowledge distillation — work that drove $300M+ in infrastructure savings and significant revenue impact. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at IIT Delhi, advised by Prof. Amitabha Bagchi and Prof. Srikanta Bedathur, working on scalable learned data structures and one-class classification. I have published in ACL, EMNLP, KDD, VLDB, WSDM, and WWW.
